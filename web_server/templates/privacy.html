Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

🔐 Overview

E-Piṭaka is an open-source project that provides access to the Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana Tipiṭaka (Pāli Canon) together with AI-assisted translations. This policy covers both the website (epitaka.org) and the companion mobile app (E-Piṭaka for Android & iOS).

We take a minimal-data approach: no accounts are required to read, no personal data is sold, and the entire source code is publicly visible so anyone can verify what we do.

📋 What We Collect & Why

Google Analytics website

We use Google Analytics (GA4) with measurement IDs G-7NQWX1DCC2 and G-MFCG30HTCQ to understand how visitors use the site — which pages are popular, how people navigate, etc. This helps us improve the experience. Google Analytics collects anonymised data such as page views, session duration, and browser type. No personally identifiable information is sent to Google.

Firebase Authentication website

If you choose to sign in (via Google or Facebook), we use Firebase Authentication to manage your session. This is optional — you can read the entire Tipiṭaka without logging in. Signing in enables features like bookmarks, reading history, and notes.

We only store your email address and display name (from your Google or Facebook profile) to associate your bookmarks and notes with your account. You can delete your data at any time by contacting us.

Local Storage website

The site stores a few preferences in your browser's localStorage so that your settings (Pāli script, font size, colour theme, disclaimer acknowledgement) are remembered between visits. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to sign in (see above).

Mobile App Android & iOS

The mobile app uses shared_preferences to store your reading preferences locally on your device — just like the website's localStorage. The app does not include any analytics SDK, tracking code, or telemetry.

The app communicates with the epitaka.org server only to fetch Tipiṭaka text data and dictionary lookups. No usage data is transmitted.

Contact email

If you send us an email at epitaka.org@gmail.com, we will keep the contents of your message and your email address only as long as needed to respond to your query. We never add you to a mailing list or share your address.

🔗 Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics — anonymised usage stats (Google's privacy policy)
  • Firebase Authentication — optional sign-in (Firebase privacy policy)
  • Google Fonts — typography (Crimson Pro & DM Sans)

None of these services receive any personally identifiable information from us beyond what is standard for their operation (e.g., your IP address when loading a page or font file).

🛡️ Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — ask us what data we hold about you
  • Rectify — correct any inaccurate data
  • Delete — request removal of your data
  • Object — opt out of Google Analytics (you can use a browser add-on)

Since almost all data is stored locally in your browser or device, you can clear it at any time through your browser or app settings. For anything stored server-side (e.g., bookmarks if you signed in), just email us and we'll take care of it.
