Why native

Everything you love about the web,
only faster.

The app is built on the same complete database as epitaka.org — so the whole Canon, its commentaries and translations travel with you, even without a connection.

Instant, offline access

The complete Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana Tipiṭaka lives on your device. Open books in a tap and read on a plane, in a monastery, or off the grid.

Root text with explanation

Read each passage with its traditional commentary just a click away, exactly like the web reader — Mūla, Aṭṭhakathā and Ṭīkā stay in sync.

Full-text search everywhere

Search passages, headings and translations across all 1,644 books — locally and without waiting on a server round-trip.

Comfortable by design

Any Pali script, adjustable fonts and layouts, text-to-speech, tabbed reading and vim-style keyboard navigation — tuned for long sittings.

Highlights

Made for real study

Inline M / A / Ṭ

Commentary (Aṭṭhakathā) and sub-commentary (Ṭīkā) linked inline to your exact position in the root text.

Any Pali script

Switch between Roman, Myanmar, Thai, Khmer, Lao, Sinhala and Devanagari instantly, paragraph by paragraph.

Powerful search

Find any passage across Pali text, translations and headings — with an integrated Pali dictionary a tap away.

Text-to-Speech

Listen while the reader follows along and auto-scrolls line by line with the spoken text.

Tabbed reading

Keep several books open at once in tabs, each remembering its own scroll position, script and settings.

Ebook exports

Prefer a PDF, EPUB or E-ink device? Every pack is also downloadable as ready-made ebooks.

Search across the entire Pali Canon — Pali words, translations and headings — right on your device.

Structured outlines with AI-generated study guides that synthesize root text, commentary and sub-commentary.
